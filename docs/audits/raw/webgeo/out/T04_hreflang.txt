WEBGEO T04.4 — HREFLANG CORRECTNESS

Sample: the five routes the brief asks for, each in all four locales.
Checked: (a) does the page emit hreflang at all; (b) are all four locales named;
(c) is x-default present; (d) is the UA prefix mapped to hreflang "uk" (the ISO
    language code) rather than "ua" (which is the COUNTRY code for Ukraine and is
    invalid as a language subtag); (e) is the page self-referential.
